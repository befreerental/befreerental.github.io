FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is the BeFree store?

BeFree Rental is located at 46 Nguyễn Đức Cảnh, Hải Châu, Da Nang. Visit the store to pick up your scooter and ask about our services, or call 0988616168.

Does BeFree offer delivery?

BeFree delivers scooters to the airport, train station, hotels and other requested locations in Da Nang. Provide your preferred pickup location and time when booking.

What is the battery-swap policy?

BeFree provides one free battery swap per day at battery-swap stations, with unlimited swaps at the store during your rental. This policy is subject to BeFree's rental terms.

What documents do I need to rent a scooter?

Vietnamese customers need a citizen ID card (CCCD); international customers need a passport. These documents are used to verify your information and complete the rental process.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

BeFree Rental does not require a deposit. Provide your personal information, complete and sign the rental agreement, then pay after signing. The process is simple, quick and convenient.

How can I contact BeFree Rental if I need assistance?

Contact BeFree by hotline at 0988 616 168, WhatsApp, Zalo, WeChat or KakaoTalk. Our team is always ready to help and answer your questions during your rental.
