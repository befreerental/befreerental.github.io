FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is the BeFree store?

BeFree Rental is located at 46 Nguyễn Đức Cảnh, Hải Châu, Da Nang. Visit the store to pick up your scooter and ask about our services, or call 0988616168.

Does BeFree offer delivery?

BeFree delivers scooters to the airport, train station, hotels and other requested locations in Da Nang. Provide your preferred pickup location and time when booking.

What is the battery-swap policy?

BeFree provides one free battery swap per day at battery-swap stations, with unlimited swaps at the store during your rental. This policy is subject to BeFree's rental terms.

What documents do I need to rent a scooter?

Vietnamese customers need a citizen ID card (CCCD); international customers need a passport. These documents are used to verify your information and complete the rental process.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

BeFree Rental does not require a deposit. Provide your personal information, complete and sign the rental agreement, then pay after signing. The process is simple, quick and convenient.

How can I contact BeFree Rental if I need assistance?

Contact BeFree by hotline at 0988 616 168, WhatsApp, Zalo, WeChat or KakaoTalk. Our team is always ready to help and answer your questions during your rental.

Contract terms
Rights and obligations of the Lessor (Party A)

Rights of Party A

  • Receive full payment of the rental fee upon motorbike handover.
  • Receive the motorbike back on time and in the same condition as delivered.
  • Require Party B to compensate for damages, lost parts, misuse, or unauthorized subleasing of the motorbike.
  • Require Party B to pay all traffic violation fines, including on-the-spot and camera-detected fines, incurred during the rental period.

Obligations of Party A

  • Deliver the motorbike of the agreed model, on time, at the designated location, in clean condition, and with all necessary accessories.
  • Bear full legal responsibility for the origin and lawful ownership of the rental motorbike.
Rights and obligations of the Lessee (Party B)

Rights of Party B

  • Receive technical support and assistance from Party A throughout the rental period.
  • Receive the motorbike of the agreed model, on time, at the designated location, and fully equipped with accessories.
  • Enjoy unlimited mileage during the rental period.
  • Get one free battery swap per day at battery-swapping stations and unlimited free battery swaps at Party A's store during the rental period.

Obligations of Party B

  • Present valid identification documents (ID/passport) and pay the full rental fee upon receiving the motorbike.
  • Inspect the motorbike carefully upon handover and preserve the motorbike and its accessories during the rental period.
  • Return the motorbike on time. In case of late return, Party B shall pay additional charges as stipulated in Article 3.
  • Strictly comply with road traffic law and be fully responsible for all traffic violation fines, including on-the-spot and camera-detected fines, incurred during the rental period, even after contract termination.
  • Not use the motorbike for illegal purposes, racing, pawning, subleasing, or allow any unqualified or unlicensed person to operate it.
  • Compensate 100% of repair or replacement costs at official or market prices for any damage or loss of parts.
General terms of both parties
  • This Contract and the Handover Minutes form an integral agreement. Both Parties commit to strictly executing all terms herein. Neither Party may unilaterally amend, suspend, or terminate this Contract without mutual consent.
  • Any disputes arising during execution of this Contract shall first be resolved through amicable negotiation. If no agreement is reached, the dispute shall be referred to a competent authority or court for resolution, and the losing party shall bear all related costs.
  • This Contract shall terminate once Party B returns the motorbike in acceptable condition to Party A and settles all outstanding fees.
  • This Contract takes effect upon signing by both Parties and is made into two copies of equal legal validity, with each Party keeping one copy.
